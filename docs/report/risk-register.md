## 1. Assessment method and ownership

Likelihood L: 1 = unlikely under current assumptions; 2 = plausible; 3 = likely without action. Impact I: 1 = local rework without a gate at risk; 2 = several deliverables/components affected or milestone threatened; 3 = mandatory submission, booking integrity, access protection or credibility of evidence compromised. Score S=L*I: 1–2 Low, 3–4 Medium, 6–9 High. These ordinal scores prioritise discussion; they are not statistical forecasts.

An owner monitors the trigger and organises treatment; collaborators keep their README responsibilities. Open means the risk is being tracked, not that an incident already occurred. A residual target is valid only after the owner supplies treatment evidence and the team reassesses it. Acceptance of residual risk is explicit; it is not implied by a lower number in this table.

## 4. Monitoring, escalation and closure

Review R-01–08 at G0–G4 on the submission day and immediately after a trigger. After confirmed submission, review weekly and at material scope/data/API changes as a proposed team practice; no automated reminders are created by this document.

For a triggered risk, record: risk ID, observed date/time and time zone, evidence link, affected gate/dependency, action owner, action due point, contingency decision and outcome. Escalate a High risk immediately to all three participants. The owner can change a score only with an explanation and evidence. Do not reduce impact merely because mitigation is proposed.

Use states Open -> Treatment in progress -> Monitoring -> Closed, or Escalated when a gate is threatened. Closure needs evidence and teammate acknowledgement; a PR merge alone is not closure. For later technical risks, agreed design can reduce uncertainty but cannot substitute for implementation tests. R-05 must be reassessed at the next real release even after this October submission succeeds.
